# Regional conventions

Use UAE / GCC when the brief does not name a region. Sizes are typical net room areas, a starting point rather than a code requirement; follow the brief when it gives sizes.

## UAE / GCC (default)

- **Entrance and guests:** main entrance into a lobby or hall; a **majlis** (formal guest living, type `living`) next to the entrance with its own **guest WC**, so guests do not pass family areas. Larger villas add a separate men's or women's majlis or a ladies' sitting room.
- **Family zone:** family living and dining away from the majlis, often opening onto a terrace or garden (`outdoor`).
- **Kitchen:** usually a closed main kitchen ("dirty kitchen") with a pantry or store; villas often add a small open "show" kitchen in the family area. Kitchen has a service door to the outside or the service yard.
- **Service:** **maid's room** (type `bedroom`, about 8–12 m²) with its own small bathroom (shower, wc, basin) next to the kitchen or laundry; laundry room; optional driver's room near the garage or entrance with its own WC.
- **Bedrooms:** every bedroom en-suite where the brief allows; master bedroom with walk-in closet and a bathroom with shower and often a tub.
- **MEP:** split or ducted AC: `ahu` in a mech / store room or ceiling space, `acu` condensers outside on a side or rear wall or roof; water heater in laundry, mech or roof; electrical DB near the entrance or garage.
- **Typical sizes:** majlis 25–40 m², family living 25–45 m², bedroom 14–20 m², master bedroom 20–30 m² plus closet 6–10 m², bathroom 5–8 m², guest WC 2–3 m², kitchen 14–25 m², maid's room 8–12 m², single garage 3.5 × 6 m, double 6 × 6 m.
- Avoid a WC or bathroom on a wall shared with the majlis where possible.

## Egypt

- Reception (`living`) near the entrance, often combined with dining; separate closed kitchen; bathrooms with wc, basin and shower (tub in the main bathroom); balconies as `outdoor` rooms; a guest WC in larger units. Apartments: bedrooms 12–16 m², reception 25–40 m².

## US (IRC)

- Bedrooms with reach-in closets (`closet`), a hall bathroom plus primary en-suite with walk-in closet, laundry room, attached garage with a door into the house (often via laundry or mudroom). Bedrooms need an egress window (IRC R310). Typical: bedroom 11–14 m², primary 16–22 m².

## International

- Follow general residential practice: clear public / private zoning, short service runs, daylight to all habitable rooms.

## Non-residential briefs

- Clinics, offices, small retail: entrance and reception first, a corridor spine, consultation or office rooms on the outer edge, WCs and a store grouped near the core, a mech room for `ahu`, `wh` and `panel`. Use `office` for consultation and staff rooms, `other` for special rooms, and name them clearly.
